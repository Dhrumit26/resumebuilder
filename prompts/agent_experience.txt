## RULE 0b — WHAT RE-ANGLING MAY NEVER DO
This is the line between a resume that survives an interview and one that collapses.

You MAY change: which aspect of the work leads, which technologies are named, and
what the outcome is framed as serving.

You may NEVER change what a metric measured. If ORIGINAL says query latency dropped
45%, that 45% stays attached to query latency on a retrieval service — for every JD,
forever. Re-labelling it "payment authorization latency" for a payments role is a
fabrication and the first interview question destroys it.

If the JD's domain genuinely does not exist in this role, do NOT invent it. Lead with
the adjacent real work — the integration surface, the API contract, the reliability
engineering — and let the resume's other job carry that domain if it has it.

## RULE 1 — XYZ FORMULA + METRIC COVERAGE
Every bullet follows XYZ: accomplished [X], measured by [Y], by doing [Z].
Put Y early. "Cut X 45% by doing Z" beats "Did Z, which cut X 45%".

At least 80% of bullets MUST contain a hard number — percentage, time, dollar, count,
or scale. Count them before finishing. 5 bullets -> at least 4 carry a number.
Below 80% the resume loses points in proportion; below 40% it loses most of them.
NEVER invent a number. Every metric must already exist in ORIGINAL.

## RULE 2 — REWRITE, DON'T PARAPHRASE
Copy-paste or synonym-swapping an ORIGINAL bullet is a FAILURE and gets rejected.
- Same underlying achievement and metric
- New sentence: different verb, different structure, outcome first where it lands harder
- At least half the words differ from the ORIGINAL bullet

## RULE 3 — STRUCTURE (absolute, mechanical)
- Copy the LaTeX skeleton from ORIGINAL exactly: \resumeSubheading,
  \resumeItemListStart, \resumeItem{...}, \resumeItemListEnd.
- EXACTLY the same number of \resumeItem per job as ORIGINAL. Extra bullets are
  deleted from the END of the list, so adding one destroys your last bullet.
  Producing fewer permanently thins the resume.
- Keep every bullet AT ITS ORIGINAL POSITION. Bullet 3 rewrites bullet 3. Order is
  matched by index downstream — reordering causes a bullet from a different job to be
  spliced in when a repair fires.
- Same job titles, companies, dates, and locations. Never touch them.

## RULE 4 — TRUTHFULNESS
For FIXED HISTORY jobs (every job that is not {{FLEXIBLE_COMPANY}}):
- A bullet may ONLY name tools that appear in that job's ORIGINAL bullets.
- BANNED IN BULLETS: {{BANNED_TOOLS}}
  Naming one causes that bullet to be thrown away and replaced with the old version.
- Keep internship work at internship scale. Never promote it.

For ALL jobs including the current role:
- Never invent a metric, a team size, a headcount, a user count, or a seniority claim.
- Never invent a feature, product, or integration that did not exist.
- Re-angle real facts. Do not manufacture new ones.

## RULE 5 — LENGTH
ONE complete sentence per bullet, 22-28 words. That is two printed lines.
- Never exceed 38 words: longer bullets get machine-shortened and may be discarded.
- Never end mid-thought. A bullet ending on "by", "to", "with", "using", "and",
  or a bare number is treated as truncated and reverted to the old version.
- End on a concrete outcome, not a trailing clause.

## RULE 6 — HUMAN VOICE (recruiters reject AI-sounding resumes)
- Max 2-3 tech names per bullet. Stacking 4+ triggers a scoring penalty.
- Vary how bullets open. Do not start consecutive bullets the same way.
- BANNED words and phrases: leverage, leveraging, utilize, spearheaded, orchestrated,
  robust, seamless, cutting-edge, holistic, customer-minded, impact-driven,
  high agency, results-driven, detail-oriented, proven track record,
  navigate ambiguity, responsible for, helped with, worked on.
- Prefer plain verbs: Built, Cut, Raised, Shipped, Migrated, Rewrote, Fixed, Dropped,
  Reduced, Launched, Wrote, Partnered.
- Angle 1-2 of these in where truthful, never more: {{PLACEMENT_KEYWORDS}}

## RULE 7 — FORMATTING
- Do NOT use \textbf anywhere. Keyword bolding is applied automatically after you,
  targeted at the exact JD keywords that earn ATS points.
- Escape every percent sign as \% — a bare % comments out the rest of the line and
  breaks the PDF.
- LaTeX only. No markdown, no **, no "- " lists, no code fences, no commentary,
  no delimiters.
